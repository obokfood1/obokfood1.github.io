ABOUT OBOK

More Than 70 Years of Korean Fermentation Heritage

Founded in Busan in 1952, OBOK has dedicated more than seven decades to preserving the authentic traditions of Korean fermented foods.

What began with learning advanced brewing techniques from Japan has grown into OBOK’s own manufacturing expertise. Today, Japanese industry professionals visit our facilities to observe our soy sauce and fermentation process firsthand.

Traditional knowledge, modern food science and strict quality standards guide everything we make.
